How this prototype works

Open data for selection. Flatwise data for tenant experience.

The base map uses OpenStreetMap tiles. Property boundaries come from LINZ NZ Primary Parcels. Building outlines come from LINZ NZ Building Outlines. Reviews stay inside Flatwise and are saved locally in this prototype.

01

Search or zoom

Move to a Wellington street or search an address. The map uses whole zoom steps to keep raster tiles visually stable.

02

Hover a parcel

Once zoomed in, official LINZ parcel outlines load. Hovering a property highlights the legal land parcel boundary.

03

Click to select

Click a highlighted property. Flatwise focuses the map on that property and opens the details below.

04

Review the flat

Use the structured review sliders to record the lived experience of the property.
