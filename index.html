How this prototype works

Map selection first. Structured reviews second.

The base map uses OpenStreetMap tiles. Optional property outlines come from LINZ parcel and building services. Reviews are saved locally in this demo, which keeps the prototype safe to test without needing accounts or a database.

01

Click a demo flat

Markers are seeded with example reviews, so the page immediately shows how Flatwise would feel with live rental feedback.

02

Rate the rental

The form uses 1–10 sliders across ten focused rental experience checks, so the score feels detailed without becoming messy.

03

Outline the property

When property lines are enabled, Flatwise tries to highlight the closest parcel outline for the selected demo flat.

04

Store locally

Saved reviews stay in the browser through localStorage. A real launch would need user accounts, moderation, privacy controls, and reporting.
