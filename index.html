How this prototype works

Boundary first. Shadow check second. Review third.

01

Load LINZ polygons

At close zoom levels, the map asks the official property boundary layer for the current view.

02

Select the property

Clicking a polygon or marker locks the review to that selected property boundary.

03

Cast 3D shadows

The 3D mode anchors building roofs to their real GIS footprints and draws building shadows as one unified tint, so overlapping shadows do not get darker.

04

Save local reviews

Tenant notes and scores stay in localStorage while the app remains a safe prototype.
